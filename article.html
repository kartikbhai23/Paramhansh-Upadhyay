Constitutional Literacy

Pan-India Legal Literacy Drive

Constitutional literacy is the bedrock of an empowered democracy. Across India, millions of citizens interact daily with administrative and judicial systems without a working knowledge of their fundamental rights under Part III of the Constitution of India. Safar Legal Trust initiates pan-India legal literacy workshops designed to translate statutory remedies into clear, actionable knowledge.

These outreach sessions demystify essential legal procedures, including First Information Reports (FIRs), consumer complaint mechanisms under the Consumer Protection Act, right to information applications, and remedies available under Article 226 and Article 32. By conducting interactive forums in colleges, community centres, and semi-urban localities, the Trust empowers citizens to navigate legal disputes with dignity and self-assurance.
